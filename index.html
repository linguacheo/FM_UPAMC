Part IV · Risk management

Rules written down before investing

Drawdown discipline
Worst back-test fall −33.7%; −40% to −50% is plausible. Treat a fall as a rebalancing signal, not an exit.
Manager trigger
Switch Ben Teng into the ETF if the manager leaves, the 3-year IR turns negative, or it lags the TAIEX two years running.
Concentration
~97% electronics makes this a bet on Taiwan tech. Add non-Taiwan equities or bonds as wealth grows.
Costs and re-planning
Rebalance only at the band edges. Three years before the home purchase, move the down payment into deposits.
The emergency reserve (≈ NT$150,000) sits outside the portfolio, so a market fall never forces a sale.
